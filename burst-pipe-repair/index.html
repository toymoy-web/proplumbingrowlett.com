A Pipe Or Valve Just Failed

Not every burst is a pipe in the wall. Many urgent calls are a broken supply valve or a split supply line under a sink or behind a toilet, where water sprays or pours until the flow is stopped.

Our 24/7 line sends a technician with the parts to stop it and repair the failed section, and the price is confirmed before work begins.

What Causes A Pipe To Burst

In Rowlett, the two most common causes are a hard freeze that catches an exposed or poorly-insulated line, and old galvanized or copper pipe that has simply corroded through after decades of service. A sudden pressure spike, often from a failed pressure-reducing valve, is a less common but real third cause worth checking once the immediate repair is done.

3

Cut out the damaged section and rejoin with the correct fitting for the pipe material.

Winter Freeze Risk In Rowlett

North Texas does not get the sustained deep freezes that force homes further north to build pipes deep underground, and that is exactly why a hard freeze here catches so many houses off guard. Exposed lines in a garage, an exterior wall with thin insulation, or an under-house crawlspace pipe in an older Rowlett home are the most common freeze points. A single overnight freeze warning is often enough to cause a break if those lines are not protected.

After The Water Is Off

Once the immediate leak is stopped, the real damage assessment starts. A burst that ran for even a short time can soak flooring, drywall, and insulation in ways that are not obvious from a quick look. We will point out anything that looks like it needs drying out or restoration beyond the plumbing repair itself, since catching that early is what keeps a burst pipe from turning into a mold problem weeks later.

If the burst also affected an area outside standard plumbing repair, cleanup and drying of saturated materials, that falls under water damage restoration rather than plumbing repair specifically, and we can point you toward the right resource for that separate piece of the job.

Codes This Work Follows

Every pipe repair or section replacement is done to the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) and International Plumbing Code (IPC) the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners adopts statewide, covering the correct fitting, material, and joining method for the pipe involved.

Cracked Pipes, Copper Repairs And When A Section Is Enough

A single cracked or split section can usually be cut out and replaced with new pipe. When a second failure follows soon after, the pipe material is the problem, not bad luck, and we tell you so.

Copper that develops pinholes or green staining at a joint is wearing out from the inside or the outside. Spot repairs buy time, and repeated ones make a repipe the sensible next conversation.

I can't find the main shutoff valve, what do I do?+

Call us immediately and describe your home's layout, we can often talk you through finding it while dispatch is already on the way. If you truly cannot locate or turn it, focus on getting people and valuables away from the water and let the technician handle the shutoff on arrival.

Will one burst pipe mean my whole house needs repiping?+

Not usually. A single burst on an otherwise sound line is a straightforward section repair. Repiping becomes the honest recommendation only when a home has had multiple burst points, or when the pipe material itself, typically old galvanized steel, is at the end of its realistic service life.
