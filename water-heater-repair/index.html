Before We Arrive

1

Cut The Power Or Gas

Switch an electric heater off at its breaker. On a gas heater, turn the gas control to off or pilot.

2

Close The Cold Water Supply

Turn the cold inlet valve above the heater clockwise. This stops new water from reaching the leak.

3

Contain The Water

Put towels or a shallow pan under the leak and move anything stored near the tank. Do not touch wet electrical components.

What Usually Causes It

Most tank failures in this area trace back to sediment buildup from hard water settling at the bottom of the tank, which insulates the burner or lower element and forces it to work harder until it fails outright. A leaking tank is different: once the steel liner corrodes through, the tank itself cannot be patched or resealed, and replacement is the only real fix.

A leak at the top of the tank, around a fitting or the cold-water inlet, is usually a loose connection or a failed T&P relief valve, and that is genuinely repairable without replacing the whole unit. Telling the two apart before quoting a price is the first thing a technician checks on site.

Tank Or Tankless

Most Rowlett homes still run a standard tank water heater, gas or electric, and repair on these is usually the fastest and cheapest fix when the problem is a thermostat, element, pilot assembly, or gas control valve rather than the tank itself.

Tankless units are becoming more common in newer builds and remodels because they take up less space and do not run out of hot water, but they fail differently. A tankless unit that starts short-cycling or throwing an error code is almost always a mineral scale or flow-sensor issue rather than a burned-out heating element.

It needs a technician who actually works on tankless units regularly, not just tank heaters.

Hard water is common across the Dallas-Fort Worth area, and mineral buildup inside a tankless heat exchanger, or settled sediment at the bottom of a tank, is the single most preventable cause of an early failure on either type. A yearly flush adds real years to either unit's service life.

Before You Call: Quick Safety Checks

  • ✓For a gas unit with no hot water, check whether the pilot light is lit before assuming the worst.
  • ✓For an electric unit, check the breaker panel for a tripped breaker first.
  • ✓If you smell gas anywhere near the unit, leave the house, call your gas utility, and do not attempt to relight anything yourself.
  • ✓If the tank itself is visibly leaking from the bottom, shut off the cold water supply valve at the top of the tank to limit water damage.

Repair Or Replace: What A Leaking Tank Means

A puddle under the tank is the most common reason people call. If the water comes from the tank body itself, the steel liner has corroded through and the unit has to be replaced, since there is no practical patch. If it comes from a fitting, the temperature and pressure relief valve, the drain valve or a supply connection, the repair is often small.

No hot water, or water that never gets fully hot, points somewhere else. On an electric unit that usually means a failed heating element or thermostat, and on a gas unit it can be the pilot, the thermocouple or the gas control. We test those before recommending anything.

Age matters in the decision. Many tank water heaters last roughly 8 to 12 years, so on an older tank that has started to leak or rust at the base, replacing it is usually better value than repairing around it.

Common Questions

Is emergency water heater repair available in Rowlett?+

Yes. We take water heater calls 24/7, and a leaking tank is treated as urgent.

If the leak comes from the tank body itself, replacement is usually the answer, and the dispatcher can tell you whether we can replace it the same day.

Can a leaking water heater actually be repaired?+

It depends on where the leak is. A leak at a fitting, valve, or connection on top of the tank is usually repairable. A leak coming from the tank body itself means the steel liner has corroded through, and no amount of resealing fixes that, so replacement is the honest recommendation.

How long does a typical water heater last in this area?+

Most tank water heaters in Rowlett last 8 to 12 years, on the shorter end of that range for homes on harder water where sediment builds up faster. A tank pushing 12 years that starts acting up is usually closer to replacement than repair.

Can a leaking water heater be repaired?+

Why is my water heater not getting hot enough?+

On electric units a failed element or thermostat is the usual cause. On gas units look at the pilot, the thermocouple and the gas control. A technician can test each in one visit.
