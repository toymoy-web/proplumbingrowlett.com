What The Ground Does To Older Mesquite Slabs

Mesquite sits on the Blackland Prairie, a band of expansive clay that swells in wet seasons and shrinks in dry ones. A house that has been through fifty years of those cycles has had a lot of chances to shift a slab or stress a line that runs beneath it. That is the plain reason slab leak detection is a regular Mesquite call, and it is the same soil story we tell in Rowlett.

The difference here is age. A Mesquite home that was built in the mid-century boom may still carry original galvanized supply pipe or cast iron drain pipe, and those materials fail in ways that newer PEX does not. Telling us roughly when the house was built before we arrive helps us bring the right parts.

Common Questions

Is emergency service available in Mesquite?+

Yes. Rowlett Pro Plumbing runs a 24/7 emergency line for Mesquite, covering burst pipes, active leaks, sewer backups, and water heater failures at any hour, including nights, weekends, and holidays.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Mesquite?+

We cover the three main Mesquite ZIP codes, 75149, 75150, and 75181. In practical terms that means the central and downtown streets, the northern side up against Garland, and the southern and eastern edge toward Sunnyvale and Seagoville.

Does the Dallas County and Kaufman County split change anything?+

Not for the license. Texas plumbing licenses come from the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE) and apply statewide, and the work itself follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) or International Plumbing Code (IPC) as the local jurisdiction adopts it. The county can matter for permits on larger jobs like a repipe or sewer replacement, so we confirm the right permitting office for the exact address first.

My Mesquite house is over fifty years old. Should I be worried about the pipes?+

Age alone is not a problem, but it tells us what to look for. Galvanized supply lines restrict flow and rust from the inside, and cast iron drains crack or scale over time. A visit that includes a look at the pipe material and a camera pass on the main drain gives you a real answer instead of a guess.
