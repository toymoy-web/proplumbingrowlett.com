Rowlett Pro Plumbing covers it as the natural next step up the Highway 78 side of our service area, about eleven miles from Rowlett.

Murphy was incorporated in 1958 and reported only 261 residents in 1970. Nearly every house in town was built in the decades since, which makes this a very different plumbing market from Mesquite or Richardson.

A Young City With Larger Homes

Because Murphy grew so recently, the typical call here is not an old pipe giving out. It is a water heater reaching the end of its life in a house built in the 1990s or 2000s, a garbage disposal that has worn out, or an irrigation system that needs its annual backflow test. Homes tend to run larger, with more bathrooms and more fixtures to maintain.

That also means the work leans toward replacement and upgrades. Our tankless water heater service and water softener pages cover the two upgrades Murphy homeowners ask about most.

Landmarks And Streets We Use To Orient A Call

Murphy Central Park, a 60-acre park with an amphitheater, a two-acre pond, and sports fields, sits near the middle of town, and the Murphy Marketplace shopping center is the main retail hub. The municipal complex, which includes the fire and police buildings and the PSA Murphy sports center, is the other easy reference point. Giving us the nearest of those three when you call helps dispatch pin down the address before the street name is even finished.

Two School Districts, One Service Area

Murphy's ZIP code, 75094, reaches into parts of Parker and Plano as well, and the city's students are split between Plano ISD and Wylie ISD. School district has nothing to do with plumbing itself, but it is a quick way for us to confirm we have the right city and jurisdiction for an address that sits near a boundary.

It also explains why many Murphy households already know our neighbors. Families here often live a few minutes from Sachse and Wylie, and their plumbing calls look a lot alike.

Lawn Irrigation And The Annual Test

Murphy's larger lots often carry a full sprinkler system, and any system connected to the home's water supply needs a working backflow preventer that is tested every year. It is a small appointment, but it is the one that keeps lawn water from ever siphoning back into the drinking supply. If you are not sure when yours was last tested, call and we will check it.

Common Questions

Is emergency service available in Murphy?+

Yes. Rowlett Pro Plumbing operates a 24/7 emergency line that covers Murphy, including burst pipes, active leaks, and water heater failures at any hour.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Murphy?+

Murphy runs mostly on one ZIP code, 75094, and we cover all of it. That includes the streets near Murphy Central Park, the blocks around Murphy Marketplace, and the pieces of that ZIP that stretch into Parker and Plano.

Do newer Murphy homes really need plumbing service?+

Yes, just different service. A 1990s or 2000s house is at the age when original water heaters, disposals, and faucets wear out together. PEX supply lines last far longer than older metal pipe, but they do not stop a water heater from aging.

Who licenses plumbers in Murphy and what code applies?+

Plumbers in Texas are licensed statewide by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE), not by the city. The work follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) or International Plumbing Code (IPC) as the local jurisdiction adopts it, and any permit for a larger job is handled by Murphy's building office.

Is Murphy part of your regular route or a special trip?+

It is regular coverage. We already run the corridor from Rowlett through Sachse and Wylie, and Murphy sits right along it.
