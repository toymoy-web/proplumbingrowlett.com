About Waterview

Waterview sits in Rowlett's 75089 ZIP code. Real-estate records tag its homes with phase names such as Waterview Phase 4B and Phase 10, and with named sections including Waterview Estates and the Villas at Waterview. Many streets carry golf names, including Augusta Lane, Hogan Drive, Pinehurst Drive, Gleneagles Lane and Saint Andrews Lane, and Waterview Parkway runs through the neighborhood.

Sales records for the area show construction years from about 1999 to 2008. A demographic profile that groups Waterview with nearby Liberty Grove describes most of its housing as built in 2000 or later. We found no official city boundary map, so we describe Waterview by its anchor streets instead of drawing exact lines.

What A House From The Early 2000s Needs Now

A house built between 1999 and 2008 is now roughly 18 to 27 years old. That is not a problem in itself. It means several parts have reached the age where replacement is a normal event, and it helps to know which ones.

A tank water heater commonly lasts about 8 to 12 years, so a Waterview house has probably had at least one replacement already. The label on the tank shows the build date, and it is the quickest way to tell whether yours is near the end. Our water heater repair and replacement pages explain how we decide between the two.

Smaller parts wear out on their own schedule. Faucet cartridges, toilet fill valves and flappers, and the braided hoses under sinks and behind toilets are all cheap to replace before they fail and messy to replace after. If your house has a pressure-reducing valve, it is a wear item too, and pressure that swings or runs high is worth a check. A faucet and sink repair visit is a good time to look at all of it.

Pools, Outdoor Kitchens And Irrigation

Listing descriptions for Waterview homes often mention pools, spas, outdoor kitchens and three-car garages, and some sections are gated. Those features add plumbing that a plain interior house does not have. A gas line to a grill or fire feature should be pressure tested if it has not been used in a while, and the gas line page covers what that involves.

An irrigation system usually has a backflow preventer that needs periodic testing. Ask the City of Rowlett whether it requires annual test reports for your address, then see our backflow testing page.

If You Think There Is A Slab Leak

If your Waterview house sits on a concrete slab, a hidden leak under it can show up as a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water when everything is off, or a water bill that jumps without a reason. A simple check is to shut off every fixture and watch the water meter. If the dial keeps moving, water is going somewhere.

Our guide to Rowlett clay soil and slab leaks explains how soil movement is thought to stress buried lines, and our leak detection page describes how we find a leak without opening walls first.

Common Questions

Which streets count as Waterview?+

Real-estate records place Waterview in ZIP 75089 and tag homes with Waterview phase names along Waterview Parkway and nearby golf-named streets. We could not find an official boundary, so if you are unsure whether your address is inside, call and tell us the street and we will go from there.

Are Waterview homes more likely to have plumbing problems?+

We have no data that says so, and we do not claim it. Age is the useful fact. A house from the early 2000s has parts that are due for replacement regardless of neighborhood, such as water heaters, fixtures and supply hoses.

How do I know if my water heater is due for replacement?+

Check the date code on the label. A tank water heater commonly lasts about 8 to 12 years. Rust at the base, water around the tank or noisy heating are also reasons to have it looked at.
