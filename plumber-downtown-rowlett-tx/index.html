About Downtown Rowlett

Downtown Rowlett grew up around the railroad. The first post office opened in 1880 under the name Morris, the Dallas and Greenville Railway reached the town in 1886, and Rowlett was incorporated in 1952. DART's Blue Line station opened in December 2012, and new building followed.

One result is the Village of Rowlett, a residential and commercial development beside the station. A 2021 DART article also described a 50-acre site at Main Street and the President George Bush Turnpike that was planned for townhomes, live-work units, apartments and commercial space.

A 2014 Dallas Morning News piece noted that the Chamber of Commerce building dates to 1923 and began as a church. Downtown is a district more than a single subdivision, and the city has not published one fixed boundary that we could find, so we describe it by Main Street and the station area.

Three Kinds Of Property, Three Kinds Of Call

Older houses and small buildings are the first kind. We do not guess pipe materials from a building's age. A camera inspection or a look at exposed pipe tells us what is actually in the ground or the wall, and our sewer line repair and leak detection pages describe how we find that out before recommending anything.

Newer apartments and townhomes are the second kind. In multifamily buildings the first question is who owns the problem. A leak inside one unit may belong to the tenant or the owner, and a shared line may belong to the building. When you call, tell us whether you are the owner, a tenant or a property manager, and who can approve the work.

Restaurants and storefronts are the third. Kitchens depend on working floor drains, grease handling and fixtures that see heavy use, and many food businesses also have backflow devices that need testing. Our commercial plumbing, backflow testing and drain cleaning pages cover that work.

Planning Plumbing Work Around A Busy Street

Downtown has event traffic, parking limits and shared alleys, so access matters. If a truck needs a particular loading spot, or a business is open during the work, tell us when you book so we can plan the visit around it. For storefronts, ask us to schedule noisy or water-off work outside business hours where that is possible.

Downtown Rowlett sits close to the Blue Line route into Garland, and we also cover Garland from the same Rowlett base.

Common Questions

Is Downtown Rowlett residential or commercial?+

Both. It includes older houses, newer apartments and townhomes, and restaurants and shops. We service all of them and ask a few questions first so the right plumber and equipment arrive.

Can you work with property managers?+

Yes, as long as the person calling can approve the work. Give us the unit or building address, who can authorize repairs and whether tenants need advance notice.

Do older Downtown buildings need their pipes replaced?+

Not automatically. A building's age does not tell us what pipe is inside it, and a leak or a slow drain has many possible causes. We inspect first and recommend a repair or a replacement only when the evidence calls for it.

Do you serve ZIP 75088?+

Yes. Downtown Rowlett is commonly associated with ZIP 75088, and our coverage includes it.
