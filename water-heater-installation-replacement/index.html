Water Heater Installation & Replacement in Rowlett

Replacing a water heater is a different job from repairing one, a full replacement means choosing the right size and type for the household, correctly disconnecting and hauling away the old unit, and installing to current code. Rowlett Pro Plumbing handles full water heater replacements across Rowlett, same day in most cases.

Hard Water And Tank Longevity In Rowlett

Moderately hard water across the Dallas-Fort Worth area accelerates sediment buildup inside a tank water heater. That is a real, measurable reason tanks here tend to sit at the shorter end of their expected 8 to 12 year lifespan rather than the longer end, especially without an annual flush. It is worth knowing when deciding between another standard tank and a unit less sensitive to sediment, like a tankless or a heat pump water heater.

When Replacement Makes More Sense Than Repair

  • ✓The tank itself is leaking from the body, not a fitting, no repair fixes a corroded tank.
  • ✓The unit is 10 or more years old and now failing repeatedly.
  • ✓You are switching sizes, gas to electric, or upgrading to a heat pump or tankless unit.
  • ✓Sediment buildup is severe enough that flushing no longer restores real performance.

Heat Pump Water Heaters, A Real Option Worth Knowing

A heat pump water heater pulls heat from the surrounding air rather than generating it directly, which uses meaningfully less electricity than a standard electric tank over the unit's life. It costs more upfront and needs enough surrounding air space to work efficiently, usually a garage or utility room rather than a small closet. That makes it a genuine, growing option worth mentioning during a replacement conversation, though it is not the right fit for every home.

Permits And Inspection

A full water heater replacement in Rowlett typically requires a permit, especially when the fuel type changes or new venting is involved, and we pull that permit as part of the job rather than leaving it as something you have to arrange separately. An uninspected replacement can become a real problem later, at resale or with an insurance claim, if the work was never permitted in the first place.

Codes This Work Follows

Every installation follows the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) and International Plumbing Code (IPC) the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners adopts statewide, including correct venting, expansion tank requirements, and temperature and pressure relief valve routing.

Common Questions

How long does a full water heater replacement take?+

Most standard tank-to-tank replacements are completed in 2 to 3 hours. A switch in fuel type, gas to electric or the reverse, or a tankless conversion, takes meaningfully longer since it involves new venting or electrical work as well.

Do you haul away my old water heater?+

Yes, removal and disposal of the old unit is included in every replacement, you will not be left with a tank to deal with on your own or need to arrange separate pickup.

Is a heat pump water heater a good fit for a garage in Rowlett?+

Often yes, a Texas garage stays warm enough most of the year for a heat pump unit to run efficiently, and the space itself is typically large enough to give the unit the airflow it needs. It is worth measuring the actual space and discussing it during your quote rather than assuming either way, since a genuinely tight closet installation is the one common exception where it does not work nearly as well.
