Not Sure Which Page You Need

A lot of calls do not map cleanly to one service name from a homeowner's side of things. A running toilet is Toilet Repair & Installation. A water bill that will not explain itself is usually Leak Detection or, if the water is under a slab foundation, Slab Leak Detection & Repair specifically. A kitchen sink that will not drain is Drain Cleaning & Unclogging. If none of the pages above look right for what you are dealing with, call (972) 903-8224 and describe the problem in plain terms and we will point you to the right fix.

Codes Every Job Follows

Every service listed above is performed to the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) and International Plumbing Code (IPC), the two codes the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners adopts statewide and tests licensed plumbers against. That standard applies regardless of which service page you came from.

Not sure which service you need? Just describe the problem on the phone.
