About Lake Shore Village

Real-estate records list Lake Shore Village streets including Lochwood Drive, Watersway Drive and Windmere Drive in ZIP 75088. Sold-home records show construction years from 2023 to 2026, so many homes here are brand new or only a few years old. Listings describe open living areas, upstairs recreation rooms and covered patios, and the community has a homeowners association.

The neighborhood is still adding homes, and we found no official boundary map, so we describe it by those streets. If your address is near them and you are not sure whether it counts as Lake Shore Village, call and tell us the street.

What To Check In The First Years Of A New Home

A new house does not need much plumbing work, but it is worth learning the system early. Find the main water shutoff, the water heater shutoff and the shutoff valve under each sink and toilet, and test that each one turns. A valve that has never been moved can be stiff or stuck the day you need it.

Watch the water meter once with every fixture off. If the dial moves, there is a leak somewhere downstream. Catching a small leak while the home is still under warranty is much easier than after it expires. Our leak detection page explains how we trace leaks without opening walls first.

Warranty First, Then Call Us

New homes usually come with builder and manufacturer warranties. Some require the builder's own repair process, and paying an outside plumber first can complicate a claim. Read the terms and make the warranty claim first if the problem is covered. Call us when the issue falls outside the warranty, when the builder cannot respond in time, or when water is actively causing damage.

Water Quality And Added Features

Some owners add a softener or filter after moving in. We do not state the hardness of any neighborhood's water here. The practical step is to test your own tap and ask your water utility for its current report, then decide. Our water softener and water filtration pages explain the options.

A newer home may also get a gas line for a grill or a second water heater later. Our gas line page covers planned work.
