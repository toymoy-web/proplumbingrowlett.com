About The Vineyards

Real-estate records place The Vineyards in ZIP 75088 and tag homes with the subdivision name on streets such as Barolo Drive, Loire Valley Drive and Merlot Circle. The neighborhood has a homeowners association, and sales records describe homes of roughly 2,400 to 4,200 square feet, with main-level primary suites, three-car garages, covered patios and, in some cases, pools or lake views.

Sources disagree on how old the neighborhood is. Sales records we found show build years from 2016 to 2021, while one neighborhood guide describes it as settled with mature landscaping. Because of that, we do not state a single build era here. If you know when your own house was built, that is the number that matters for plumbing. We also found no official boundary, so we use the anchor streets above.

Bigger Houses Mean Longer Pipe Runs

A larger house often puts the water heater a long way from the farthest bathroom. That means a long wait for hot water, more water wasted while you wait, and more pipe that can leak. A recirculation pump, a second water heater or a tankless unit are the usual answers, and which one fits depends on the layout. Our tankless water heater and water heater replacement pages explain the tradeoffs.

Large homes also tend to have more fixtures running at once. If hot water runs out when two showers and the dishwasher are going, the heater may be undersized for the house instead of failing.

Gas Lines, Pools And Outdoor Features

If your Vineyards home has a gas pool heater, grill line, fire feature or generator, each of those is a gas connection that should be tested whenever work is done nearby. If you ever smell gas, leave the house first and call 911 or your gas utility before calling us. Our gas line page covers planned work.

Homes with irrigation have a backflow preventer that needs periodic testing. Ask the City of Rowlett whether it requires annual reports, then see our backflow testing page.

Check The Warranty Before You Pay For A Repair

If your home was built in the last several years, a builder or manufacturer warranty may still apply to part of the plumbing. Some warranties require the builder's own repair process, and paying an outside plumber first can complicate a claim. Read the warranty terms, then call us if the problem falls outside them or cannot wait.

Common Questions

Which streets are in The Vineyards?+

Listing records show Barolo Drive, Loire Valley Drive and Merlot Circle in ZIP 75088. We found no official boundary, so call with your street name if you are not sure your address is included.

How old are Vineyards homes?+

Sources disagree. Sales records we found show 2016 to 2021 build years, and a neighborhood guide describes mature landscaping. Your own closing documents or the county appraisal record will give the real date for your address.

Why is hot water slow to reach some bathrooms?+

In a large house the water heater can be far from the farthest tap, so cold water in the pipe has to clear first. A recirculation pump or a second heater can shorten the wait, and we can look at which option fits the layout.

Does my builder warranty cover plumbing?+

That depends on the warranty. Check its terms before paying for a repair, because some require the builder to do the work.
